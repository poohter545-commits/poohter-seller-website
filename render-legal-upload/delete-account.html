What Happens Next

  1. Poohter support receives your request and may verify that you own the seller account.
  2. After verification, we disable or delete the seller login and remove personal profile details where possible.
  3. Requests are normally reviewed within 30 days.

Data We Delete

We delete or de-identify seller profile information, login data, verification uploads, shop details, and product/media data that is no longer needed for active marketplace operations.

Data We May Keep

We may retain order, payout, tax, accounting, security, fraud-prevention, dispute, or legal records where required by law or legitimate marketplace safety needs.
